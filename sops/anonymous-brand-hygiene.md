# SOP: Anonymous Brand Hygiene

## Purpose

Ship and market a product without leaking personal identity when you want the brand to stay anonymous.

## Before you export the buyer ZIP

Search the whole product folder for:

- Your real name and common nicknames
- School / employer names
- Personal domains and old project names
- Social handles and emails tied to you
- Local file paths that include your username
- Screenshots with desktop clutter or personal bookmarks

Fix or remove hits. Re-zip. Search again.

## Accounts

- Use a product-only email
- Gumroad / GitHub / social: product brand name, not your face if you are staying anonymous
- Do not reuse bio text from your personal profiles

## Writing voice

- Prefer "I" for a solo builder story, or neutral voice ("this kit")
- Do not invent a fake team
- Do not invent fake credentials

## Support

- Answer as the product brand
- Keep personal Zoom calls off the table if anonymity matters
- Async email / form only

## Teaser repos and posts

- Check commit author name/email on public repos (use a brand git identity)
- Check image EXIF if you are careful about location metadata
- Read the README once as a stranger looking for clues

## When anonymity is NOT needed

Skip this SOP. Named brands convert fine. Use this only when anonymity is a deliberate choice.
