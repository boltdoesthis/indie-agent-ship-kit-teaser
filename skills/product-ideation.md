# Skill: Product Ideation

## Use when

You have energy to build but no clear paid offer, or you have five half-ideas and need one you can ship in 7 days.

## Goal

Pick one digital product idea that is specific, sellable on Gumroad, and buildable with an agent in under a week.

## Inputs you need

- Audience you already reach or can reach (even small: 50 people, a Discord, a niche subreddit)
- Skills or assets you already have (docs, scripts, checklists, design files, domain knowledge)
- Hard constraint: max build time (default 7 days) and max price band (default $19–$49)

## Steps

1. **List raw constraints (5 min)**
   - Write: audience, time budget, price band, format limits (PDF, Notion, ZIP of markdown, code pack, spreadsheet).
   - Reject anything that needs a mobile app, ongoing SaaS hosting, or a team.

2. **Generate 8 idea seeds**
   - Each seed = audience + painful job + deliverable format.
   - Example shape: "Freelance designers who lose hours on client intake → a Cursor skill pack + intake form templates."
   - Prefer problems you have solved for yourself in the last 90 days.

3. **Score each idea (1–5) on**
   - Pain clarity (would they pay to remove it?)
   - Speed to v1
   - Distribution fit (where will 20 people see it?)
   - Differentiation (not another generic "AI prompts" dump)
   - Keep top 3.

4. **Pressure-test the top 3**
   For each, answer in one sentence:
   - Who buys on a Tuesday night?
   - What do they download and open first?
   - Why is this better than a free blog post?
   Kill any idea that fails the "better than a free blog post" test.

5. **Choose one and write the offer card**
   ```
   Name:
   Buyer:
   Job to be done:
   Format:
   Price:
   7-day build outline (Day 1…Day 7):
   Success = first 3 buyers or 10 waitlist emails
   ```

6. **Stop and get human approval** before drafting sales copy or building files.

## Output format

- Table of 8 seeds with scores
- Top 3 with pressure-test answers
- One approved offer card

## Anti-patterns

- "AI tool for everyone"
- Ideas that need a big audience before day 1
- Bundling 12 products into one vague mega-pack
- Skipping the free-blog-post test
